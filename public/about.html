Our Story

Established in 2013, incoporated in Dec 13 2014, Temivic Suntechnology was created with a clear purpose: to provide professional and dependable solutions for air-conditioning, refrigeration, and climate-control challenges.

Over the years, we have evolved alongside industry technologies. From residential split-unit air conditioners to complex commercial installations and industrial HVAC systems, we understand that effective repair and maintenance require deep technical knowledge, proper diagnosis, and an understanding of the entire system.

Our Engineering & Technical Standards

  • Accurate Diagnosis: Identifying root causes before executing repairs to eliminate recurring failures.
  • Correct Equipment Selection: Matching cooling capacity, efficiency ratings, and operating environment specifications.
  • Proper Installation Practices: Executing positioning, drainage, electrical hookups, and airflow tuning correctly.
  • Preventive Maintenance: Proactively servicing equipment as an investment to extend asset service life.
  • System Efficiency: Ensuring systems not only cool, but operate with minimal energy consumption.
  • Accountability & Documentation: Providing detailed service history and technical records for complete transparency.
